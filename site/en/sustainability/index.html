Planet

Take action today for a better tomorrow.

We are committed to implementing environmentally sustainable business practices and investing in new technologies to reduce the environmental impact of our operations.

  • Green Resources

    We invest in green energy. The Novadelta industrial unit already runs 100% on renewable energy and is optimized with natural light, LEDs, and automated systems.

  • Solar Panels

    32% of the energy needs of our commercial infrastructure are already met by photovoltaic solar panels — and installation continues.

  • Electric Fleet

    We are electrifying our commercial fleet. In Lisbon, it is already 100% electric, with 150 vehicles.

We stand for Biodiversity

Protecting the ecosystem means caring for the land that sustains us. When coffee grows in harmony with nature, everyone benefits.

Healthy Soils

Taking care of the soil ensures the future of coffee.

(01)We promote organic coffees that use fewer chemicals, helping to preserve and regenerate the soil.
(02)We encourage sustainable farming practices among small-scale producers.
(03)We prioritise production methods that safeguard soil fertility and minimize erosion.

Coffee& Climate

Collaborating for a fair climate future.

Since 2010, Delta has been part of this initiative to ensure that coffee production is more sustainable and resilient to climate change.

2010 - 2016

Phase I

Development and testing of the Coffee & Climate approach, creating practical methodologies to assist coffee producers in addressing climate challenges.

2016 - 2019

Phase II

Expanding the initiative in the pilot countries aims to scale effective practices and expand the network of partners and technical expertise.

2020-2023

Phase III

Encouraging coffee producers to adopt more sustainable practices, focusing on reducing emissions and enhancing the carbon storage capabilities of coffee plantations and their surrounding landscapes.

2024 - 2028

Phase IV - In progress

Evaluation of coffee-based agroforestry systems and the implementation of climate strategies with a beneficial impact at the local level.
