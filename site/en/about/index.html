Delta Principles

These are the principles at the heart of Delta.

Great legacies are built on enduring beliefs. These four define ours.

  • (1/4)

    Proximity

    Every customer is unique and important. "One customer, one friend" is the motto that guides us from north to south and across borders, fostering genuine connections and greeting familiar faces.

  • (2/4)

    Humanism

    People come first. Working with a community must represent a meaningful contribution to local well-being. We stand by our word — and prove it through our actions.

  • (3/4)

    Authenticity

    Tradition is measured by quality. We honour what is best, and we evolve in full view of everyone. From the first to the last sip, the experience must remain genuine.

  • (4/4)

    Trust

    Six decades of strong relationships show us that respect and consistency are always reflected back by clients, partners, and communities. We can expect the best because we do not hide our best.
